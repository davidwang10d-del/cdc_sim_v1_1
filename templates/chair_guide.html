CDC Chair Guide

This page is for chairs and crisis staff with no coding background. Keep it open during committee.

Normal launch: double-click START_CDC_SIM.bat, wait until it says the server is running, then open http://127.0.0.1:5000.

1. Start Of Committee

  1. Open the dashboard in the browser.
  2. On the first screen, click the scenario card you want to run.
  3. Wait until the map appears. Do not press Play yet.
  4. Click MUN. Read the committee name, rules, roles, and faction readiness.
  5. Click Saves, then click Save here on Checkpoint 1. This manually records your clean starting point.

2. During Debate

Delegates debate only

Keep the simulation paused. Use the map as a reference board.

Delegates submit directives

Crisis staff reads the directive, decides the effect, then enters it in the MUN panel.

Military movement happens

Select a unit, choose Relocate, Planned Route, or Assault, click the map destination, review the plain-language behavior, then click Authorize.

3. Operational Orders

  • Hold: stop and keep the current combat stance.
  • Relocate: move directly without starting ranged combat; the unit still defends itself at close contact.
  • Planned Route: click several waypoints, then authorize once. The unit holds ranged fire while moving.
  • Assault: advance more slowly, stop at detected enemy contact, fight, and resume if contact clears.
  • Defend Here: stop, recover, and dig in while retaining the selected stance.
  • Fortify: build fieldworks faster by spending supply and gaining fatigue, then automatically defend.

The combined Operational Order panel contains two independent choices. Task says what the unit should do and where. Combat Stance controls fire discipline, speed, concealment, recon, and combat modifiers. Changing either keeps the other. Relocate and Planned Route always withhold ranged fire; close-contact self-defense remains active. Defend and Fortify build fieldworks in any stance.

4. How To Adjudicate A Directive

  1. Click MUN.
  2. In Public update / directive title, write a short title such as Supply convoy reaches Kabul.
  3. In the large text box, write one or two sentences that chairs are comfortable reading aloud.
  4. In Faction or GLOBAL, type the exact faction name. Use GLOBAL if all factions are affected.
  5. Choose the effect:
    • No mechanical effect: story/news only.
    • Morale +/-: speeches, propaganda, battlefield shock, political legitimacy.
    • Supply +/-: logistics, sanctions, aid, fuel, ammunition, food.
    • Fatigue +/-: overextension, rest, weather, disease, poor planning.
    • Combat multiplier: temporary training, advisors, weapons, poor coordination.
    • Speed multiplier: bridges, roads, airlift, sabotage, difficult terrain.
  6. Enter Value. Examples: +5 morale, -10 supply, 1.2 combat multiplier, 0.8 speed multiplier.
  7. For multiplier effects, set Duration hours. For morale/supply/fatigue, duration is recorded but the change is immediate.
  8. Leave Publish to delegate-facing crisis feed checked for public updates. Uncheck it for private staff notes.
  9. Click Apply Adjudication.

5. Good Ruling Sizes

Use small effects often. Use large effects rarely.

  • Minor successful directive: +/- 3 to 5 morale or supply.
  • Strong, detailed directive: +/- 8 to 12 morale or supply.
  • Major strategic success or disaster: +/- 15 to 25 morale or supply.
  • Temporary modifier: 1.1 or 1.2 for a bonus, 0.8 or 0.9 for a penalty.

6. Save, Reset, And Export

There is no autosave. Commands, chair edits, and resets remain only in memory until you explicitly save a checkpoint.
  1. Save before every major crisis update: click Saves, then click Save here on an empty checkpoint or Overwrite on an existing one.
  2. Each checkpoint shows its save date, simulation time, unit count, and whether it is compatible with the mounted DLC.
  3. If something goes wrong, click Restore on the last good checkpoint. Confirming replaces the current state and leaves the simulation paused.
  4. To return to the original scenario, click Reset scenario. This does not create a backup.
  5. To export a summary, click MUN, then Export SITREP. A Markdown file downloads.

7. Build Your Own DLC

  1. Click Editor on the main dashboard.
  2. Fill the tabs from left to right: Scenario & Map, Simulation Rules, Committee, Diplomacy, Units, Battalion Types, Weapons, Export.
  3. Use Battalion Types to create infantry, armor, artillery, militia, engineers, special forces, or custom unit building blocks.
  4. Use Simulation Rules for battle-specific unit types, capabilities, and tuning. Tactical orders and combat stances are shared core doctrine and appear read-only.
  5. Click Check Package.
  6. Click Export DLC Zip.
  7. Unzip the package into the project dlc folder and refresh the dashboard.

8. Common Problems

  • No scenario appears: check that the scenario folder is inside dlc and contains scenario.json.
  • The browser cannot connect: make sure START_CDC_SIM.bat is still open.
  • You want to check the installation: open http://127.0.0.1:5000/status. It lists missing dependencies or invalid DLC packages.
  • Map loads slowly: wait. DEM terrain files are large.
  • An airdropped unit does not appear: choose at least one battalion and confirm deployed personnel is greater than zero. Every battalion must have a positive size in its DLC data.
  • A unit will not bombard: it must receive the bombard capability named by the active DLC rules, have enough supply, and target a point in range.
  • You made a bad ruling: restore the previous manual checkpoint and re-enter the ruling.
Chair principle: CDC Sim is an adjudication aid, not the committee itself. Chairs should use it to keep rulings consistent, but political judgment and crisis pacing still come from staff.
